Instant Replay now starts a fresh clip after each save. Save a two-minute replay, wait ten seconds, and save again to get about ten seconds of new footage.

- The replay counter resets to 0:00 when a save starts. ReplayLite keeps recording while it writes the file.
- If saving the MP4 fails, the buffered footage stays available for another attempt.
- Manual recording can continue through replay saves.

A live capture test produced a 10.00-second second clip and confirmed the retry and manual recording behavior.
